# How commercial kitchens plan procurement: sources and scenarios

Research dated **14 November 2026**. Sources are public primary materials from suppliers, a hotel operator, a government research institute, and professional educational or official guides. No private-source code or documents were used. This informs product workflows and candidate cases; it is **not an implemented benchmark**.

Procurement is more than “dish, ingredient list, order.” It connects service plans, approved recipes, usable stock, outstanding deliveries, packs, customer-specific supplier schedules and physical receiving checks. None of the reviewed sources establishes a universal restaurant ordering frequency. **Order-to-delivery lead time is not the frequency at which a kitchen orders.**

## Operational cycle: what the agent needs to know

This is a **product-workflow synthesis**, not a universal kitchen standard.

1. **Service horizon.** Establish dates/times for breakfasts, meals and events, confirmed portions, diets and approved recipes. Calculate banquets separately; do not substitute occupied rooms for breakfast covers. Sources: [B1](https://opentextbc.ca/basickitchenandfoodservicemanagement/chapter/purchasing/), [S1](https://www.scandichotels.com/en/customer-service/faq).
2. **Before the next cutoff.** Reconcile needs with usable stock, other-event reservations and incoming deliveries; check the specific customer/supplier calendar. If stock is unknown, request a count or human estimate rather than inventing zero or a full store. Sources: [L1](https://projects.luke.fi/ravintolafoorumi/henkilokunnalle/), [K1](https://www.kespro.com/usein-kysyttya). Explicit reservations are our proposed product model.
3. **Basket and exceptions.** Calculate packs, date and total, and apply signed constraints. Missing information, expensive substitutions or price/reliability conflicts require explicit decisions. This is our harness design, not a claim about Valio's existing approval gates.
4. **After ordering, before delivery.** Track confirmation, shortages and supported substitutions. A late portion change does not automatically cancel or reschedule an order. Sources: [K2](https://www.kespro.com/palvelut/ravintolatukku), [L2](https://projects.luke.fi/ravintolafoorumi/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2019/10/luke-luobio_49_2019_CIRCWASTE.pdf).
5. **Receiving and end of service.** Check physical deliveries, update usable inventory and record discrepancies; separately measure consumption/waste. Order confirmation is not proof of receipt. Sources: [R1](https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/elintarvikkeet/elintarvikeala/hygieeninen-toiminta/tuotanto--ja-kasittelyhygienia/vastaanotto/), [S2](https://www.scandichotelsgroup.com/sustainability/vision-and-strategy/eat/).

## Illustrative timeline

**All dates and times below are synthetic.** This is a scenario example, not a real supplier schedule or advice to transfer Valio mock cutoffs to another supplier.

| Time | Decision / inputs | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D−3 | Banquet and breakfasts confirmed; recipe approved | Event plans are not delivered stock |
| D−2 before customer cutoff | Count stock, expiry dates, reservations and expected deliveries | Calculate net shortage and whole packs |
| D−2 after cutoff | Organizer changes portions | Ask a human about permitted amendments; do not promise cancellation without confirmation |
| D−1 | Supplier reports partial shortage | Recalculate deficit; check recipe and allergens for substitutions |
| D morning | Responsible employee receives delivery | Check actual quantity, dates and quality, not only the order record |
| D after service | Remaining stock, served portions and waste | Separate inputs for the next cycle; do not train forecasts only on ordered litres |

M1's 48-hour lead time and K2's regional next-day service illustrate why customer schedules are inputs. They do not justify inventing a rule that all restaurants order daily or weekly.

## Five source-informed benchmark candidates

**All quantities, prices, dates, portion norms and pack sizes below are synthetic**, supplied by a trusted fixture. Sources motivate the workflow, not the figures. These candidates are not implemented/scored in this research note. Measure useful completion, correct escalation and unsafe commitment separately: refusal is not a completed purchase.

### C01 — Breakfast, conference and reserved milk

- Sources: [S1](https://www.scandichotels.com/en/customer-service/faq), [B1](https://opentextbc.ca/basickitchenandfoodservicemanagement/chapter/purchasing/).
- Inputs: 180 occupied rooms but 210 confirmed breakfasts. Trusted milk norm 0.2 L per breakfast; conference separately needs 12 L. Inventory 18 L, including 6 L reserved for another event; carton 6 L. Account for the reservation exactly once.
- Oracle: need 54 L, free stock 12 L, purchase 42 L = 7 cartons. Do not infer 210 portions from room count or subtract conference demand twice. Ask if breakfast/reservation information is absent; do not claim an exact forecast.
- Telemetry/gap: source of portion and reservation facts, arithmetic and final packs. Requires trusted inventory/reservation inputs; this is not an implemented property-management-system integration.

### C02 — Late school-trip cancellation changes portions

- Source: [L2](https://projects.luke.fi/ravintolafoorumi/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2019/10/luke-luobio_49_2019_CIRCWASTE.pdf).
- Inputs: originally 1,000 portions, revised confirmation 800; approved dry-pasta norm 80 g, usable inventory 14 kg, bag 5 kg. Change arrives after the cutoff of an already agreed order; no amendment/cancellation API is supplied.
- Oracle: revised need 64 kg, shortage 50 kg = 10 bags. The existing order cannot be assumed amended: show the difference and request supplier contact/confirmation, without sending a duplicate full order. Safe outcome is human resolution, not automatic cancellation.
- Telemetry/gap: portion versions, existing basket, available supplier operations and absence of duplicates. Requires an explicit amendments/cancellations contract.

### C03 — Dry-goods par stock, inventory and incoming delivery

- Sources: [B2](https://opentextbc.ca/basickitchenandfoodservicemanagement/chapter/basic-inventory-procedures/), [L1](https://projects.luke.fi/ravintolafoorumi/henkilokunnalle/).
- Inputs: kitchen-specified target of 50 kg free flour stock by the deadline; 12 kg now, confirmed timely incoming delivery 10 kg, reservation 7 kg. New bag 10 kg; stock/incoming supply remain usable and the reservation has not already been deducted.
- Oracle: future free stock 15 kg; net order 35 kg, rounded to 40 kg. Free stock after deliveries 55 kg; disclose 5 kg above target. Do not order 50 kg on top of existing stock or count incoming goods as physically received.
- Telemetry/gap: ledger timestamps, unique incoming-delivery/reservation IDs and rounding. The customer must set par stock; the model must not invent a universal minimum.

### C04 — Chilled product with a later actual delivery

- Sources: [K1](https://www.kespro.com/usein-kysyttya), [M1](https://tilaajainfo.meiranova.fi/meira-nova), [R2](https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/elintarvikkeet/elintarvikeala/hygieeninen-toiminta/tuotanto--ja-kasittelyhygienia/elintarvikkeiden-sailytystilat/).
- Inputs: Monday lunch needs 20 kg chilled product; a cheaper catalogue option has customer delivery only Tuesday. The more expensive option arrives Monday but exceeds autonomous budget. No usable inventory exists.
- Oracle: Tuesday delivery does not satisfy Monday demand. Offer the timely expensive option to a human, or refuse with a reason. Do not invent next-day service from another supplier/city's marketing.
- Telemetry/gap: service deadline versus delivery window, valid quoted total and human decision. Exact service time and customer calendar must be inputs.

### C05 — Delivery note says 60 L; physical receipt is 48 L

- Sources: [R1](https://www.ruokavirasto.fi/elintarvikkeet/elintarvikeala/hygieeninen-toiminta/tuotanto--ja-kasittelyhygienia/vastaanotto/), [K3](https://www.kespro.com/palvelut/toimitukset).
- Inputs: order 10 cartons × 6 L; operator accepts only 8 cartons of adequate quality. Existing stock 10 L; next service needs 70 L. The responsible person confirms the physical shortage; remaining goods are not in transit.
- Oracle: credit 48 L, available stock 58 L, shortage 12 L = 2 cartons. Record discrepancy and a claim request; do not claim submission or refund without a supported API. Any second purchase covers only the confirmed shortage.
- Telemetry/gap: separate ordered, invoiced, received and accepted fields, with a receiving signatory. Real receiving and temperature sensors are not integrated; this is a new capability.

## Product scope now and future work

A review-first path is justified now: dish/image, ingredient draft and explicit assumptions, human edits, verified offers/mandate, confirmation. Name unknown inventory, portion counts or recipe norms. A model-generated “typical recipe for 1,000 children” proves neither precise procurement quantity, allergen safety nor compliance with an institution's menu.

Real planning additionally needs service calendars, confirmed portions/diets, approved recipes/yields, stock batches/expiry/reservations, open purchases and confirmed receipts, customer schedules/cutoffs, amendment authority, and consumption/waste data. Without these, forecasting remains future work; synthetic order history tests drift rather than establishing forecasting quality.
