Four signed documents

Ed25519, JWS, JWKS

  1. Buyer mandate

    The buyer signs what its agent may purchase, its limits and who can approve exceptions.

    valio.mandate.v1

  2. Signed offer

    Valio signs price, availability, delivery and validity for each quoted product.

    valio.offer.v1

  3. Cart confirmation

    A person signs the exact cart hash. Exceptions can still need a separate approval.

    valio.cart_confirmation.v1

  4. Order receipt

    Valio signs the committed order and the audit head at the time of placement.

    valio.receipt.v1
