# Initial scope and evidence gaps

This is an initial scope stub for the project-local kitchen-workflows task, not a claim
that a new paper-aggregation stage was executed. The earlier project reading list is in
[real-procurement](../real-procurement/research_internet.md); its eight listed papers
already include Malefors etal.2022 (DOI10.1016/j.resconrec.2021.105997). The new pass
focuses on primary workflow documentation, rather than repeating that review. No paper
assets were downloaded and no managed ARF lifecycle commands were run.

## Gaps and Limitations

* G1: Which ordering horizons and customer/supplier delivery windows are actually
  documented, and how do order frequency and lead time differ?
* G2: Where do school, restaurant, hotel, hospital meal-order and wholesale
  ingredient-order workflows diverge?
* G3: What trusted inputs are needed for recipe scaling, usable stock and dietary
  constraints, and what cannot safely be inferred from a dish name or photo?
* G4: Which grounded cases are implemented, which remain blueprints, and which useful
  outcomes require clarification or human approval?
* G5: Is there representative procurement-error frequency or a real demand/inventory
  dataset with which to validate this project's forecast or business-impact claims?
